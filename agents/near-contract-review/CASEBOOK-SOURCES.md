# Sources of the case-book (knowledge/cases-*.md)

The entries of the case-book are restatements in our own words of classes of findings seen in the public audit reports listed below; no sentence or code of a report is reproduced, and the reports about the contracts held out for testing (Octopus nep141-token-convertor, nep141-token-vesting and appchain registry; Ref Finance xREF and ref-ve; Stader NearX; Aurora staking-farm; the SWEAT token, Defer and Burn Model; Mintbase; cNEAR; Potlock) were not opened and not used.

Where the reports are public: BlockSec's at https://github.com/blocksecteam/audit-reports/tree/main/rust,
Guvenkaya's at https://github.com/Guvenkaya/public-reports, Halborn's at
https://github.com/HalbornSecurity/PublicReports and https://www.halborn.com/audits, Trail of Bits' at
https://github.com/trailofbits/publications, the Templar reports in the project's repository
(https://github.com/Templar-Protocol/contracts/tree/main/audits), the Wormhole report in
https://github.com/wormhole-foundation/wormhole/tree/main/audits, and most of the others in the collection
https://github.com/NEARBuilders/audits.
