What happens to your money

  1. 1It waits in the contractOn NEAR, under the hash of your task. Nobody can take it: not the swarm's owner, not its runner.
  2. 2The swarm answersExperts find, a second expert tests each finding, an arbiter settles disputes. Within 30 minutes.
  3. 3The contract pays and returnsYou pay the rates of the experts that delivered; the contract shares them among those experts by what their findings that held were worth. What your task did not cost goes straight back to your wallet. An unused spare costs nothing.
  • Declined, whether refused, out of scope or failed on our side: all of it comes back but 0.05 USDC, the check of your task.
  • Not answered in 30 minutes: anyone can call refund, and all of it comes back.
  • The contract keeps hashes of your task and of the answer, never their text. Both are shown only to you. The hash is bound to your account: copied into another account's order, it gets nothing.
  • One answer, no reworks or disputes: you pay only for the parts that passed the checks, and inside the answer every finding was already disputed by a second expert and an arbiter.
  • Settled by the contract. Accounts are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Coming soon on Agent Market, with two rounds of changes and the market's arbiter for disputes.
