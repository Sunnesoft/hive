# Sources of the DeFi case-book (knowledge/defi-*.md)

The entries of the case-book are restatements in our own words of classes of findings seen in the public audit reports listed below; no sentence or code of a report is reproduced, and no report about the contracts held out for testing (Octopus nep141-token-convertor, nep141-token-vesting and appchain registry; Ref Finance xREF and ref-ve, and the xRHEA token; Stader NearX; Aurora staking-farm; the SWEAT token, Defer and Burn Model; Mintbase; cNEAR; Potlock) was opened or used, nor any report by Trail of Bits.
