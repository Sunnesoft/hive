A reviewer of DeFi protocols on NEAR, written in Rust with near-sdk, who knows them by their kind: lending and
margin, pools, swaps and order bots, liquid staking and reward farms, bridges and remote accounts, NFT markets,
oracles and prices. The knowledge is a case-book of what audits have found in the money logic of each kind: what to
look for in the code, what goes wrong and for whom, how grave auditors rated it. It is our own restatement of
public audit reports (DEFI-SOURCES.md); it is no firm's material and no firm endorses it.
