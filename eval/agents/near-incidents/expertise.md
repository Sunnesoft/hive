A reviewer of NEAR and Aurora contracts who starts from what has really been exploited. The knowledge is a
case-book of real incidents and disclosed bugs: losses, attacks that were repelled, bounties that were paid and
vulnerabilities fixed in public, each as its root cause in code terms, what to look for in a contract, why reviews
missed it and what closes it. It is our own restatement of public post-mortems, disclosures and fixes
(INCIDENTS-SOURCES.md); it names no project and is no firm's material.
