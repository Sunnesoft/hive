# National Games Board of Sorvia: guidance notes

Guidance Note 2023/4, draws made by a smart contract. A contract may make the draw of Art. 14 only if its random
value comes from a verifiable random function or a public randomness beacon. Block timestamps, block hashes,
difficulty or prevrandao values and the caller's address are not an independent source: the block producer or
the caller can choose or predict them. A game drawn from them does not comply with Art. 14, and its result may be
set aside on a participant's complaint within 30 days.

The same note on money held by a contract: the share that the contract keeps for the organiser is "costs" under
Art. 8 and falls under the 12% cap, unless the contract itself sends the rest to the stated purpose. A contract
that lets anyone enter cannot meet Art. 10 by itself: the organiser needs an allow-list of verified adult
members, or sells entries through a front end that checks age and membership.

Guidance Note 2022/2, municipal registries. A notification under Art. 5 is complete when it has the purpose, the
ticket price, the prize plan, the responsible person and the planned dates. The registry has 10 days to object;
silence is consent. The estimate of yearly sales in the notification is binding: an organiser that passes
90,000 EUR stops sales and applies for a licence.

Guidance Note 2024/1, households. Where an organiser limits tickets per household under Art. 12, one address
counts as one household, and the limit is published with the method of the draw.
