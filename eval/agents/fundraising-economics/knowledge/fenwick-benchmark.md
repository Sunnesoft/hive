# Fenwick benchmark of community raffles, 2024 edition

Sample: 212 raffles, 2019 to 2024, housing estates and villages of 500 to 3,000 households, monthly draws.

## Participation and tickets by ticket price

| ticket price | households that buy at least one ticket in a draw | tickets per buying household | sales per 1,000 households per draw |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 EUR | 47% | 2.9 | 4,089 EUR |
| 5 EUR | 42% | 2.5 | 5,250 EUR |
| 8 EUR | 27% | 1.8 | 3,888 EUR |
| 10 EUR | 19% | 1.5 | 2,850 EUR |

The price that brings in the most is 5 EUR. Sales scale with the number of households: an estate of 2,000
households at 5 EUR sells 2,000 x 42% x 2.5 x 5 = 10,500 EUR a draw.

## What holds participation up

The figures above are for raffles that publish each draw's result. They hold for the first 12 months; in the
second year participation falls by about a fifth unless the purpose changes.

## How often

Weekly draws bring 1.6 times the yearly sales of monthly draws, not 4.3 times: households buy fewer tickets per
draw. Monthly is the benchmark's base case.

## Costs

Median cost of running a raffle: 9% of sales. Payment fees 2.9%, printing and notices 3.1%, an independent count
3.0%. Raffles sold only through an app ran at 6%.

## Prize plans

Median payout: 52% of sales. One main prize of 60% of the prize money with many small prizes sells 12% more
tickets than winner-takes-all. Prizes in kind from local shops sell no worse than cash.

## Subscriptions

Where the law allowed standing monthly subscriptions, they raised yearly sales by 22%: subscribers do not drop
out between draws.

## Seasons

December draws sell 30% more than the yearly average, July and August 20% less.

## Matching

31% of the raffles that asked local businesses got a match of 0.50 EUR for each EUR that reached the purpose,
usually capped at 10,000 EUR a year.

## Alternatives, same communities

One-off appeal: 35% of households give, median gift 40 EUR. A levy voted by the members: 96% pay, but a levy
above 30 EUR per household was voted down in 7 cases of 10. A sponsored event day: a median of 6 EUR per
household.

## Projects

Playgrounds in the sample cost a median 12% more than their budget.
