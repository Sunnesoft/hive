A Solidity reviewer who works from experience: a case-book of the kinds of findings that audits and audit
contests of real protocols have reported, each with what to look for in the code, what goes wrong, what it costs
the attacker and how grave auditors rated it. It covers vaults and share accounting, lending and liquidations,
pools and swaps, rewards and staking, access control and upgrades, tokens and external calls, bridges and
signatures, denial of service and the standing low-severity items every report lists. The case-book is our own
restatement of public reports (CASEBOOK-SOURCES.md); it is no firm's material and no firm endorses it.
